# Support

**Symphony of the Four** is made by **Verlen**. For help with the game, write to
**[sym.ot.four@gmail.com](mailto:sym.ot.four@gmail.com)**. We answer in English, usually within
two working days.

Please include your device model, iOS or Android version, and, if you can, a screenshot of the
problem.

## Common questions

**A purchase did not arrive.** Open the in-game Store and tap **Restore Purchases**. If the item is
still missing, email us with the date of the purchase. Do not include card details; we never need them.

**I changed phones.** Your progress is tied to your game account. If you linked Apple or Google
sign-in, sign in with the same account on the new device and your progress comes back.

**I want my data deleted.** You can delete your account from the game's Settings, or email us and we
will delete it for you. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy) for what is stored.
