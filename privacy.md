# Privacy Policy

**Symphony of the Four** is published by **Verlen** ("we"). This policy explains what the game
collects, why, and how you can delete it. Effective date: 8 September 2026.


## What we collect

- **An anonymous account identifier.** On first launch the game creates a random identifier on
  your device and registers it with our game-service provider, Microsoft PlayFab. It is not your
  name, phone number, or email. If you later link a sign-in (Apple or Google), the provider
  gives us a stable account reference so your progress can be restored on a new device.
- **Your game progress.** Base layout, towers, heroes, currencies, quest and ladder progress,
  season passes and cards you hold. This is the data that makes the game work across devices.
- **Purchase records.** When you buy something through the Apple App Store or Google Play, the
  store sends us a receipt so we can grant the item and prevent it being granted twice. We never
  see your card or payment details; the stores handle payment.
- **Gameplay telemetry.** Events such as battle start and end, tutorial steps, purchases made,
  defeat reasons and crash reports, tied to the anonymous identifier. We use them to balance the
  game and fix problems.
- **Content you write.** A display name, guild name and guild message, if you set them. Other
  players can see them; they are filtered for abusive language and can be reported or blocked.
- **Technical data.** Device model, operating system version, app version, language and rough
  region, sent with telemetry and crash reports.

We do **not** collect precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. The game
contains no advertising network.

## Why we use it

To run the game and keep your progress; to deliver what you bought; to detect cheating and abuse
in ranked play (our service verifies battles it is sent); to fix crashes; to balance content.
We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

## Who sees it

- **Microsoft PlayFab** (account and progress storage) under Microsoft's privacy terms.
- **Apple and Google** for purchases and, if you link one, sign-in.
- **Unity Technologies** for crash reporting (Unity Cloud Diagnostics), if enabled in the build.
- Our own game service, which verifies battles and stores the economy ledger.

## How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. Telemetry is kept in aggregate; raw event logs are rotated.

## Deleting your data

In the game: **Settings → Account → Delete account**. This erases your progress and the
identifier from our service and from PlayFab. Purchases cannot be restored after deletion.
You can also email the address below.

## Children

The game is rated for ages 12 and up (PEGI 12). It contains no loot boxes or paid random
rewards. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13; if you believe a
child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU/EEA and the UK under the GDPR) you may ask for a
copy of your data, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing. Use the in-game deletion
or the contact below.
